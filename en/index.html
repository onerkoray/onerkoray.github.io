In English

Koray Öner

Koray Öner (also written Koray Oner) is a freelance software developer in Türkiye. He builds calculators for Turkish payroll, income tax, social security (SGK) and pensions that derive every figure from the legislation in force, and writes the articles that explain the rules behind them.

One question sits at the centre of the work: how do you know a calculation is right? On this site the answer is a system, not a promise. Every figure is computed from legal parameters, recomputed on every change and published only after it passes its tests. The site is in Turkish; this page is an overview in English.

Method

How the numbers are checked

Most online calculators give you a number without saying where it came from. When the law changes, nobody tells you: the tool keeps working and simply gives the wrong answer. The site is built to rule out both problems structurally.

  • Legal parameters in one place

    Income tax brackets, the minimum wage, the social security ceiling and the severance ceiling live in a single file. A check stops any calculator or article from carrying its own copy: a copy is what someone forgets to update.

  • Article figures are tested too

    Every amount in an article is recomputed in a test and compared with the value on the page. Tables are parsed from the HTML and checked cell by cell; a single broken cell fails the build.

  • Dated claims expire

    A sentence such as "not yet in force" writes its own recheck date into the page. When the date arrives the build turns red; the article does not wait to be noticed.

  • The tests are tested

    After a new test is written, deliberate errors are planted in the code to measure whether the test really catches them. A test that does not is decoration.

  • Primary sources

    The Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete), the Revenue Administration (GİB), the Social Security Institution (SGK), TurkStat (TÜİK) and the Central Bank (TCMB).

Experience

From enterprise software to statutory calculations

Over his career Koray Öner has worked on many projects building professional tools for organisations, in Türkiye and abroad. The habit those projects leave behind: in enterprise software an error is usually silent. The screen opens, the table lines up, only the result is wrong.

The verification system on this site is that habit applied to personal finance. He now works freelance and is open to project collaboration and open-source contributions.

Interfaces and accessibility
HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework or server dependency. Keyboard use, screen reader support and WCAG colour contrast are measured on every build.
Calculation engines and testing
Engines derived from the legislation that call one another; tests that read a figure off the page and recompute it; deliberate-error runs that test the tests.
Data pipelines
Automation that pulls TCMB, TÜİK and World Bank data every night, compares the sources with one another and turns them into charts.
Technical SEO and performance
Structured data, sitemaps and date consistency; page weight and loading behaviour. Each is pinned by a check.
Other areas
Backend and API design, DevOps and automation, artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Principles

Publishing principles, in brief

The full text and the corrections log are on the publishing principles page (in Turkish).

Independence
Calculators and articles are free, ad-free and need no sign-up. No sponsored content, no link sales, no guest posts.
Sources
Every claim sits next to a primary source and a check date. Source rule
Corrections
An error that is found is logged with the date of the correction, never changed silently. Corrections log
Privacy
Calculations run in your browser; the amounts you enter do not leave your device. An invoice archive or Financial Twin profile you choose to save stays in your own browser. Privacy
AI
AI agents help write code, drafts and charts. They are not a source of facts: the figures are recomputed by tests, and the responsibility is Koray Öner's. Disclosure
Open code
The source of the site and of the calculation core is on GitHub under the MIT licence.

The calculators are for general information only. They do not replace advice from a certified accountant, a lawyer or an official authority.

Calculators

Where to start

Selected calculators. The pages are in Turkish; the numbers speak for themselves.

  • Gross-to-net salary

    Monthly payroll for every month of the year, with income tax brackets, the SGK ceiling and the minimum-wage exemption; payroll years 2020–2026.

  • Payslip audit

    Enter your payslip lines and see which deduction differs from the expected amount, and why.

  • Severance and notice pay

    Severance (kıdem) and notice (ihbar) pay with the current statutory ceiling.

  • Retirement pension

    How contribution days and earnings turn into a monthly pension under the SSK and Bağ-Kur rules.

  • Retirement date

    Earliest retirement date from your first insurance date, age and contribution days.

  • Tax bracket creep

    How much extra income tax your wage pays because the brackets rose less than wages.

  • Annual income tax return

    Income tax on rent, freelance and multiple-employer income, with the declaration thresholds.

  • Employer cost

    What a gross or net salary costs the employer, including SGK and unemployment insurance shares.
